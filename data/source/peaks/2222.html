Tinkham Peak's twin rocky summits liue along the Cascade crest just south of Silver Peak. The topographic map labels the east peak with the "Tinkham Peak" label and provides a spot elevation of 5395 feet, but the west peak is slightly higher and holds the Mountaineers trail register. A survey using photo-pixel analysis indicates that the west peak is most likely about five feet higher.


On the summit of Tinkham Peak, with Silver Peak (5,605 feet) in the background. The day was hot and sunny, and skies were hazy and smoggy due to smoke from forest fires in British Columbia (2017-08-08). Photo by Ken Akerman.
