The South Peak of Cardinal Peak was measured by differential GPS in 2023 and is about 7 inches higher than the Middle Peak. On this site, climbers that reached only the traditional middle summit are still receiving credit for the peak, due to the closeness of the two peaks in elevation and distance. The summit marker is now place on the South Peak. Climbers should, ideally, visit both peaks to be 100% sure they touched the highest rock.
