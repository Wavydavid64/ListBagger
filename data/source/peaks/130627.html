In 2023, using several accurate surveying methods, Eric Gilbertson determined that this craggy summit is 3 feet higher than the traditional, easier summit of Snoquamie Mountain reached by trail. Therefore, this peak is the highest and most prominent on the Snoqualmie massif, even though the vast majority of hikers do not do the difficult scramble over to this rocky tower.



This site will continue to show the traditional Snoqualmie Mountain summit as the listed peak for "club lists" that are subjective lists of random peaks, such as the Mountaineers Peak Pins and the Smoot book list (but if the Mountaineers formally announce a change, it will be shown here).



Other lists, based on objective criteria such as the Alpine Lake Home Court, have been updated with the new higher summit.
