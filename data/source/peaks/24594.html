Other Photos


Church Mountain from the lookout site. It's a bit difficult to make out in this photo, but the route follows a shelf that's roughly level with the trees adorning the gendarmes from this point of view, then takes a sharp right up the gully with a small snow patch near the top. From there, the route contours behind the East summit, then (apparently) ascends directly up the ridge to the true summit (2024-07-04). Photo by Sam Beaumont.
