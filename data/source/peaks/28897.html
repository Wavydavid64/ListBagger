Named after the genus name for raven.

Don Duncan used a surveyor's level to determine that this summit is 17 feet higher than Libby Peak (the 8580 foot summit to the west), with an estimated error of plus or minus two feet. See the November 2000 Issue of the Mountaineer newsletter.

Eric Gilbertson on the summit during a January ascent (2019-01-26). Photo by Jake Robinson.
